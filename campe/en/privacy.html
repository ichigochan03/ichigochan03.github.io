Privacy Policy

CamPe

This policy explains how Ichigo Channel (“we”) handles your information in the iPhone app CamPe (“the App”).

1. Information the App Handles

The App doesn’t collect personal information such as your name or email address. The following information is stored only on your iPhone and is never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Scripts you type or paste
  • Settings such as text size and scroll speed
  • Videos and audio you record

2. Use of Device Features

  • Camera: To record video and show a preview while recording.
  • Microphone: To record sound in your videos.
  • Speech Recognition: To scroll the script as you speak. Your speech is converted to text on your iPhone, and neither your voice nor the text is sent anywhere.
  • Photo Library (add only): To save recorded videos to the Photos app. The App never reads your existing photos or videos.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads and measure their performance, Google may collect information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Device information such as IP address, device type, OS version, and language
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped

Your scripts and recorded videos are never sent for advertising.

You can change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you don’t allow tracking, you can still use the App, but the ads won’t be based on your interests.

For how Google handles information, see:

  • Google Advertising Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

4. In-App Purchases

Payment for the in-app purchase that removes ads is handled by Apple’s App Store. We never receive payment information such as credit card numbers.

5. Links to Other Sites

The App includes links to our YouTube channel and X (formerly Twitter). Information on those sites is handled under each service’s own policy.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact @ichigochan_nel on X.

Effective date: November 6, 2026
